# Hi, I'm Omar 👋

**Data Platform Engineer at BAMA · Oslo, Norway**

[Website](https://omarmassfih.no/) · [Engineering notes](https://omarmassfih.no/notes.html) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/omarmassfih/)

</div>

I build data platforms on **Azure and Snowflake**, from ingestion and transformation pipelines to the infrastructure that runs them. At BAMA, I work with **Python, dlt and dbt**, automate infrastructure with **Terraform and CI/CD**, and build agentic AI solutions with **LangGraph and LangChain**.

Before BAMA, I worked at **IBM** on backend services, APIs, AI systems and **Kubernetes/OpenShift** platforms. I have a master's in **Informatics: Programming and System Architecture** from the University of Oslo.

The part I enjoy most is the platform underneath: how data moves, how services recover, and how to make the whole thing easier for the next person to build on.
